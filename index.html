Seven modules

Everything on the same record

Every module reads and writes on the same employee file. None of them duplicates another's data, so there are never two versions of the truth.

Employee file and org chart

Personal data, position, area, contract, status and document file. The reporting line between manager and employee is declared once and drives every approval.

Employee self service

Each person checks their own employment information without writing to anyone: profile, attendance, vacation balance, benefits and payslips.

Attendance

Absences, punctuality, worked hours and overtime. Visible to HR and to the employee, over the same record.

Requests and approvals

Vacation, leave and permissions with traceable states. Each request is routed to the right manager according to the declared reporting line.

Benefits and vacation

Bonuses, gift baskets, vouchers and leave. Accrued and used days update on their own when a request is approved.

Payslips and payment status

A repository by period for the payslips HR uploads, with the status of each deposit in plain sight.

Workplace wellbeing

Temperature, lighting and air quality by space, classified against the thresholds each organization defines.

Requests

Nothing gets lost in a chat

Every request has a state, an owner and a date. Whoever submitted it knows where it stands without asking, and whoever approves it resolves from the notification.

01Pending

In progress

The employee submits it. The system finds their direct manager in the org chart and assigns it to them.

02Under review

Under review

The approver asks for the missing information and the request goes back to the employee without losing its history.

03Approved

Approved or rejected

It is resolved with a recorded reason. If it consumes balance, the vacation balance updates right away.
