Terms of service

Last updated: 01/09/2026

These terms govern the use of Flowboard, the HR management platform operated by Performily. By subscribing to the service, the client organization accepts the conditions described below and undertakes to pass them on to the people it registers in the platform.

1. Purpose of the service

Flowboard centralises employment record information: employee file, organizational structure, attendance, benefits, payslips and requests. The platform does not calculate salaries, does not apply deductions or contributions, and does not replace the payroll or accounting system the organization already uses.

2. Accounts and credentials

Credentials are generated when each employee is registered and require a password change on first sign in. Every account is personal and non transferable, and the organization is responsible for keeping the list of people with active access up to date.

3. Permitted use

The service is intended for managing the staff of the contracting organization. Using it to process information about people outside the employment relationship, reselling access, or attempting to obtain information that does not correspond to the user profile is prohibited.

4. Responsibilities of the client organization

The organization is responsible for the accuracy of the information it uploads, for the correct assignment of roles and reporting lines, and for keeping the documents that labour regulations require in their original format.

5. Availability and support

Performily works to keep the service continuously available and announces scheduled maintenance windows in advance. Incidents are handled through the channels listed in the contact section.

6. Term and termination

The service is contracted for renewable periods. When the contract ends, the organization may request an export of its information within the agreed window, after which the data is deleted from Performily systems.
