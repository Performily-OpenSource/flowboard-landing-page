Privacy policy

Last updated: 01/09/2026

Performily processes the personal data of employees registered in Flowboard in accordance with Peruvian Law 29733, the Personal Data Protection Law, and its regulations. This policy describes what data is collected, for what purpose, who can access it and how to exercise the rights the law grants to the data subject.

1. Data collected

Identification and contact details, employment record information, attendance records, requests, and the documents the organization uploads to the employee file.

2. Purpose of processing

Managing the employment relationship inside the client organization. The data is not used for advertising purposes and is not shared with third parties outside that purpose.

3. Access by role

Each employee accesses only their own information. Human Resources staff access the information of the organization. A manager accesses the requests of the people who report to them.

4. Retention

Data is retained for the duration of the employment relationship and for the period that labour regulations require after it ends.

5. Rights of the data subject

Access, rectification, cancellation and objection, exercised before the employing organization in its capacity as owner of the personal data bank.
